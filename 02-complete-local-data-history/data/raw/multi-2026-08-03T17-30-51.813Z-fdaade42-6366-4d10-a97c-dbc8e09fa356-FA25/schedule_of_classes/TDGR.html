Theatre and Dance


The Theatre and Dance Department administrative offices are located in Galbraith Hall. Check the online Schedule of Classes for updates prior to going to the first class session.

Students who do not attend the first day of class may not be allowed to continue in the class. It will be the responsibility of those students to drop themselves out of the class. Failure to drop will result in a grade of "F" being assigned.

Multiple Courses Search Tips
  • Search for a range: MATH 10-20, LISP 1-3
  • Search for a range including letters: POLI 100A-DA
  • Search for an individual course (separated by commas,
    spaces or returns): POLI 27, DOC 1, MUS 15
  • Search for your entire schedule: WCWP 10A-10B, POLI 27,
    MAE 100-198, MATH 20A-F, POLI 100DA, PHYS 2A-2CL
  • Remember: Specifying the subject code once without a
    range will not work: CSE 10, 13, 14, 19, 27
