Please refer to the Physics Department Enrollment Policies: https://physics.ucsd.edu/students/undergraduate/ enrollment-policies Check your @ucsd.edu email and VAC for announcements regarding your schedule. Exceptions will not be made for students who fail to receive/read UCSD email and/or VAC notifications. Students who enroll in any of the following courses will be charged a lab fee which will be assessed at the time of registration: PHYS 1BL ($3), PHYS 1CL ($7), PHYS 2CL ($5), PHYS 2DL ($25), PHYS 120 ($25), PHYS 122 ($25), PHYS 124 ($25), PHYS 133 ($50)
