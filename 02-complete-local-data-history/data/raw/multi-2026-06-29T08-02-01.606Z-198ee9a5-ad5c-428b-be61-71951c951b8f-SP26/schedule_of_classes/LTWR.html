Literature


The Literature Department teaches courses in literatures from many areas of the world. World literature courses taught in English translation may be found under the following subject headings:
LTAF (Literature/African)
LTAM (Literature of the Americas)
LTEA (Literatures/East Asian)
LTEU (Literature/European & Eurasian)
LTWL (Literatures of the World)
For detailed course descriptions, please visit: http://literature.ucsd.edu/ Please see the department for more information.

Literature/Writing (LTWR )


LTWR 1**C are craft/reading courses, LTWR 1**W are writing workshops, and LTWR 140-149 are writing theory courses. Declared Literary Arts (LT42) and Literature/Writing (LT34) majors and LT34/LT39 minors Have priority enrollment for these courses. It is strongly recommended that declared LT42 and LT34 majors and LT34 and LT39 minors enroll in no more than 1 writing workshops per quarter. Students who are not declared majors or minors may put in an authorization request via the Enrollment Authorization System (EASy). Requests for authorization will be processed during second-pass. Requests are only processed M-F 8am-4:30pm so please plan accordingly.
